Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 August 2026

1. On-device processing

By default, your PDFs are processed entirely on your device. Viewing, editing, signing, page tools, and on-device conversions do not send your documents anywhere.

2. Conversion service

When you use an Office↔PDF conversion, the selected file is uploaded to our conversion server solely to perform the conversion, and is not retained after the conversion completes.

3. Collaborate

When you create a share, the selected PDF and any comments are stored on our collaboration server, addressed by an unguessable token, until you delete the share. Anyone with the link can view the document and its comments.

4. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. The app receives only your entitlement status; it never sees your payment details.

5. What we do not collect

No account is required. The app does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.
